# Bitblock

A 3D block-building survival game that runs right in your web browser. Open `index.html` to play.

## Controls
- **WASD** move · **Space** jump / swim · **Ctrl** sprint
- **Mouse** look · **Left click** mine / attack · **Right click** place, eat, use, open
- **1–9 / mouse wheel** pick a slot · **E** inventory and crafting
- **N** map · **P** photo album · **M** sound on/off · **Esc** pause

## Features
- A huge world (100,000,000 × 100,000,000 blocks) with biomes, caves, ravines, mountains, oceans and flowing water
- Survival: health, hunger, day and night, zombies, crafting and tools
- Towns with villagers, wizard towers and libraries; pyramids, temples, ancient cities, mineshafts, shipwrecks and ruined portals
- Magic scrolls and books, spell combining, fire, wizards, cameras and movies
- Three worlds: the first world, the **Crystal Realm** floating 200 blocks above (reached through Sparkle portals), and the **Ember Depths** below (reached through Fireball portals)
